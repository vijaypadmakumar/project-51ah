Scientific temper, humanism,inquiry and reform

Project 51 A(h) approaches each project as an evolving process of discovery. Through observation, material exploration, testing, and refinement, ideas are developed in response to the specific conditions and possibilities of each commission.

Inspired by Article 51A(h) of the Indian Constitution, the practice embraces scientific temper, humanism, and the spirit of inquiry and reform as an approach to thinking and making.
